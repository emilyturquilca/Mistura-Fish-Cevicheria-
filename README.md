# Mistura Fish · Operations and reports

The first release covers purchasing, suppliers, receiving, inventory, waste, recipe costing, profitability and Loyverse synchronization. Production, kitchen orders, staff, customer loyalty and forecasting are reserved for the second phase.

See [the Spanish user guide and setup instructions](README.es.md).

The buildless frontend calls authenticated Vercel Functions. PostgreSQL (Neon) stores a versioned JSONB operations document. Server validation and an atomic version comparison prevent lost updates; command IDs prevent duplicate writes on retries. Receipts and catalog data synchronized through the server are shared across devices. APP_PASSWORD, LOYVERSE_TOKEN and DATABASE_URL must be configured server-side. No secrets are committed.

Enter ingredient category, supplier, unit, stock and unit cost in one form. Receive purchases to increase stock and record ingredient waste to reduce it. API-synchronized sales automatically consume configured recipe ingredients once, from activation and recipe creation onward; recipe revisions preserve historical formulas. Financial refunds do not replenish prepared ingredients, and discarded refunded dishes can be logged without a second deduction. Reservations aggregate recipe requirements and shortages without reserving stock. Hourly sales use local receipt time and export 24 numeric rows to Excel. Historical Loyverse profit and theoretical recipe margins are presented separately. Manual spreadsheet imports remain device-local.

Use Node.js 22+, npm ci, and npm test. node tests/preview.mjs runs an explicitly marked local fixture with synthetic data and in-memory storage; it does not test the live database. Connect a separate database/branch for preview testing. The API creates its initial table on authorized access without inserting business fixtures. See the Spanish guide for first-release storage design and limitations.

Operational records can be edited and deleted with inline Undo or recovery from Settings. Purchase deletion includes linked receipts and movements, with a preview of stock effects. Restoring preserves posting order. Corrections replay inventory valuation and later outgoing costs; absolute stock counts retain their physical total. Referenced suppliers/ingredients and invalid manual stock changes require correcting their dependencies first; actual POS sales can expose shortages as negative stock.

Supplier profiles link purchase history, ingredients, delivery ratings, quantities and price changes to receipt-based payables. Cash terms declare the received amount fully paid, exclude it from debt lists and retain it in supplier history; credit terms remain payable until manual payments settle them. Calculated cash coverage counts only the remainder after existing manual payments, so totals and history do not duplicate payments or invent a payment method. Switching a receipt back to credit removes that coverage and retains manual payments. Existing receipts without payment terms still require confirmation. Manual payments retain date, overpayment and dependency validation, and support editing and recoverable deletion. Receipt document and lot fields are optional and explain their purpose.
